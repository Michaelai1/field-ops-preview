How It Works

Three steps.

No app to download. No training required. Crews scan the QR code on the machine and submit — the office and shop see it immediately.

01

Scan the QR on the Machine

Crew scans from any smartphone — no app download, no login. The form opens instantly, pre-loaded with the asset ID.

02

Submit the Request with Photos

Select the request type — maintenance, transport, inspection, or daily log. Add required photos and a description. Hit submit. Done in under a minute.
